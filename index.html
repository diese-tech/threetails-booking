Before You Visit

What this is. Three Tails Cafe is a managed cat lounge. You're reserving a 60-minute visit with our resident cats — many of whom are adoptable through our local rescue partners. Light beverage service may be available; the cats are the experience.

Heads up. Cat dander is present throughout the lounge — please skip your visit if you have a serious cat allergy. Photos are welcome, but please no flash. We aim to be accessible; if you have a specific need, contact us before your visit and we'll do our best to accommodate.

Questions before you book? Call (352) 432-8028 or email dustin.nieves@threetailscafe.com
  • Our cats come first. Please be gentle, move slowly, and let the cats approach you — never chase or corner them.
  • Sanitize your hands before entering the lounge. Hand sanitizer is available at the entrance.
  • Use inside voices. Cats have sensitive hearing — please keep noise low and silence your phone in the lounge.
  • Children ages 13 and under must be supervised by an adult at all times, with no more than 2 children per adult in the lounge.
  • No outside food or drinks in the lounge. Please do not feed the cats — they are on special diets.
  • The lounge cats are residents in partnership with local rescues. Personal pets are not permitted; only certified medical assistance animals are allowed in the cafe area.
  • Do not wake sleeping cats. They need their rest too!
  • Full payment is collected online at the time of booking. Refunds are available if cancelled at least 24 hours before your visit.
  • Need to reschedule? You can update or cancel your reservation from the link in your booking confirmation.
