📋Before You Visit

  • 🐱Our cats come first. Please be gentle, move slowly, and let the cats approach you — never chase or corner them.
  • 🧼Sanitize your hands before entering the lounge. Hand sanitizer is available at the entrance.
  • 🔇Use inside voices. Cats have sensitive hearing — please keep noise low and silence your phone in the lounge.
  • 👶Children 13 and under must be accompanied by an adult at all times. No more than 2 children per adult in the lounge.
  • 🚫No outside food or drinks in the lounge. Please do not feed the cats — they are on special diets.
  • 🐶No personal pets allowed inside. Only certified medical assistance animals are permitted in the café area.
  • 😴Do not wake sleeping cats. They need their rest too!
  • 💰This booking is a reservation confirmation only — payment is collected at the café upon arrival.
  • 🔄Need to reschedule? Please contact us at least 24 hours in advance at dustin.nieves@threetailscafe.com or call 123-456-7890.
